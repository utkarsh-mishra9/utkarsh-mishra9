Utkarsh Mishra, Dr. Jane Smith, Dr. John Doe

International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML) 2024

This paper presents novel machine learning approaches for analyzing complex systems data. Optimization Algorithms for Large-Scale Network Problems

Utkarsh Mishra, Dr. Sarah Johnson

Journal of Computational Science, Vol. 45, 2023

We propose new optimization algorithms specifically designed for large-scale network problems. The algorithms demonstrate superior scalability and convergence properties compared to traditional approaches. Applications to real-world network optimization problems show promising results with significant computational speedups.

Data Mining Techniques for Pattern Recognition in Time Series

Utkarsh Mishra, Dr. Michael Brown, Dr. Lisa Wilson

IEEE Transactions on Data Mining, 2023

This work explores advanced data mining techniques for identifying patterns in time series data. We develop a hybrid approach that combines ensemble methods with feature engineering to improve pattern recognition accuracy. The proposed method has been validated on multiple real-world datasets with excellent results.

Statistical Models for Predictive Analytics in Healthcare

Utkarsh Mishra, Dr. Emily Davis

International Conference on Healthcare Informatics, 2022

We present statistical models tailored for predictive analytics in healthcare applications. The models incorporate domain-specific knowledge and handle the unique challenges of healthcare data including missing values, temporal dependencies, and privacy constraints. Clinical validation demonstrates the practical utility of our approach.
